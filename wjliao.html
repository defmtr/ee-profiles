  • Prof. Wen-Jiao Liao received B.S. degree in electrical engineering from the National Taiwan University, Taipei, Taiwan in 1995. He received the M.S. and Ph.D. degrees in electrical engineering from the Ohio State University, Columbus, in 1999 and 2003, respectively. In 2007, he joined the faculty of the Department of Electrical Engineering, National Taiwan University of Science and Technology, where he is now a Professor. He received the certificate from the IEEE Antennas and Wireless Propagation letters for his exceptional performance as an Article Reviewer from 2018 through 2021. Since May 2019, he has been appointed as associate editor of IET Microwaves, Antennas & Propagation. He was awarded as distinguished professor from NTUST on 2021 and currently serves as the department chair. His main interests include miniaturized antennas, diversity antennas, electronically reconfigurable antennas, phased arrays, antenna measurement, wave propagation, and stealth technique.
