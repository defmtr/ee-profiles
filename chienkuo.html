研究領域Research Areas
  • (1) 高電壓工程、局部放電、絕緣劣化與破壞試驗。
  • (2) 資料探勘、機器學習、深度學習等人工智慧於故障診斷之應用。
  • (3) 再生能源與儲能於電網之動態、穩態與情境分析。
  • (4) 演算法於電力系統智慧化調度與運轉控制之應用。
  • (5) 感測器(電場/磁場/超音波/紅外線)、濾波器(電路/數位)、資料蒐集、邊緣運算、雲端計算與展示。
  • (6) 鋰電池劣化與破壞之檢測、靜電場量測與消除、非破壞性檢測。
  • (1) High voltage engineering, partial discharge, insulation degradation and breakdown tests.
  • (2) Application of artificial intelligence in fault diagnosis : data mining, machine learning, and deep learning.
  • (3) Dynamic, steady-state and situational analysis of renewable energy and energy storage in the power grid.
  • (4) Application of algorithms in intelligent dispatch and operation control of power systems.
  • (5) Sensors (electric field, magnetic field, ultrasonic, infrared), filters (circuit, digital), data collection, edge computing, cloud computing and display.
  • (6) Detection of lithium battery degradation and damage, electrostatic field measurement and elimination, and non-destructive testing.
